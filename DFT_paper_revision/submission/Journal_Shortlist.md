## What the manuscript is, after revision

A bounded bulk DFT+U comparison (48-atom cell, Γ-only, one dopant each) of oxygen-vacancy energetics and hole localisation for a trivalent rare-earth and a monovalent alkali substituent in anatase, with an explicit electron ledger and sensitivity tests. No experiment, no interface or device calculation. Scope words for the search: *defect chemistry, DFT+U, doped TiO~2~, oxygen vacancy, charge compensation, polaron*.

## Recommendation

1. **Submit to Applied Surface Science** once the Part E items 1–4 of the response document are closed (dual-U cycles, D3 parents, k-mesh table, spin density). These take roughly one week of compute on the stated hardware and remove the audit's two remaining "critical" scientific gaps (R06, R13).
2. If Applied Surface Science returns the manuscript on scope, transfer to **Journal of Alloys and Compounds** (same Elsevier submission system; the cover letter needs only the fit paragraph changed).
3. Hold **Journal of Materials Chemistry A** for a second paper that adds a matched-valence dopant series (La/Y/Sc, K/Na) and corrected charge-transition levels; the present evidence base does not support a JMCA-level claim.
4. **Physical Review Materials** or **J. Phys. Chem. C** are appropriate if the authors prefer a physics/physical-chemistry readership over impact factor, or if UM accepts Scopus-list Q1.
