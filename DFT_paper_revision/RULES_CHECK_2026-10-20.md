**F2 — should-fix: Ch.5 Introduction should be about 10–15% of total word count**  
Where: Section 1 (l.19–27) relative to Sections 2–6 (l.29–235)  
Problem: The Introduction is 6.4% of the main text, against the handbook's 10–15%. The cause is the length of Methods and Results, not a short Introduction: they carry run-level provenance that belongs in the SI.  
Evidence: Introduction 750 words. Main prose 11,808 words (13,010 in the docx including tables and captions), giving 6.4% (5.8%). Methods 2,769 words (23%), Results 5,661 (48%). Individual paragraphs of 619 (l.127), 785 (l.187) and 842 words (l.231).  
Fix: Do not pad the Introduction. Let it grow to about 830 words through fixes F5 and F7, and move about 3,000–3,500 words of run-level provenance from the main text to the SI, so the main text drops to roughly 8,500 words and the Introduction reaches about 10%. Candidates and current lengths: §2.4 (379 words: per-run smearing list and −TS values), §2.5 (364: starting-magnetisation bookkeeping), §2.6 (318: force histories), §2.8(iii) (179: HSE input flags), §2.8(vi)–(viii) (237), §3.3 paragraph 1 (619: run numbers 4/24/25, occupation remarks), the end of §3.6 from 'The Kohn–Sham gap of Rb_VO from the non-self-consistent eigenvalues ...' to the paragraph end (unconverged eigenvalues, Fermi-energy mesh remarks), the §3.7 k-mesh (466) and Bader (359) paragraphs, and §5 (842 words, much of which repeats the §3.7 'Uncertainty of the main results' paragraph). Leave one-sentence pointers to the SI tables in their place.

Refs [12] and [16] are both cited earlier in the paragraph, so the numbering order is unchanged.
