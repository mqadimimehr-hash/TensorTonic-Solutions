# ML From Scratch: Content Plan

Plan for turning ML-from-scratch practice into tutoring and paid content.

## Important: stay original
TensorTonic's problem statements, test cases and official solutions belong to them.
**Don't copy or sell them.** Teach the *algorithms* (softmax, linear regression, …) in your own
words, with your own code and examples. Those are general ML knowledge and are yours to teach.

## Format for each topic
Each topic folder has three parts:
1. `explainer.md`: written lesson (blog, Medium, GitHub, or study-guide chapter)
2. `short-video-script.md`: 45–60 s TikTok/Reels/Shorts script
3. `*.py`: clean, runnable code

## How it earns money (in order)
1. **Weeks 1–4: build an audience (free).** Post 2–3 short videos per week and put the explainers on
   GitHub/Medium. The goal is proof that you explain things clearly.
2. **Tutoring (fastest income).** Offer 1-on-1 or small-group ML/Python help to UM and other students.
   Use Fiverr, Upwork, Preply, or local student Telegram/WhatsApp groups.
   Set your price to what students in your market pay, then raise it as reviews come in.
3. **Paid study guide.** Once you have ~10 topics, bundle the explainers + code + practice questions
   into a PDF, "ML Algorithms From Scratch: Exam & Interview Prep", and sell it on Gumroad,
   Ko-fi or Payhip. Keep 2–3 topics free as samples.

## Weekly routine (~5 hours)
- 1 h: solve/understand one algorithm
- 1.5 h: write the explainer + code
- 1.5 h: record & edit one or two short videos
- 1 h: post, reply to comments, reach out to potential tutoring students
